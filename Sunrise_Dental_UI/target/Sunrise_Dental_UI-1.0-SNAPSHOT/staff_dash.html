Help

Quick guide for staff
  1. Appointments — View all registered appointments and use the built-in search bar to filter records instantly.
  2. Register Appointment — Fill out and submit patient booking details directly within the dashboard.
  3. Dentists — View, add, update, or remove dentist profiles dynamically with real-time search support.
  4. Staff Management — Search and view all staff members. Admin users can create, edit, or remove staff accounts.
  5. Calculate & Print Bill — Enter an appointment number to calculate total cost and print clean receipts.
  6. Notices — Select a dentist and send a notice or message directly to them.
  7. Profile Settings — View your account details and update your name, contact number, or password.
