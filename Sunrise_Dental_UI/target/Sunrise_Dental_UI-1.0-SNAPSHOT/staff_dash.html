Staff Portal Guide

Click on any topic below to see more details
Appointments View and manage every registered appointment
Lists every appointment across the clinic with patient details, dentist, treatment, date, time and status. Use the search bar to instantly filter by appointment number or patient name, and click + Add Appointment to book a new slot straight from this page.
Register Appointment Book a new patient visit in a few steps
Enter the patient's name, address and contact number, then choose the dentist, treatment type, date and time. Click Submit & Save Appointment to confirm the booking, or Cancel to discard the form without saving.
Dentists Manage clinic dentist profiles and login accounts
Search dentists by name or specialization, or click + Add New Dentist to create a profile with their specialization, contact number, consultation fee and login credentials. Each row's Actions let you edit or remove that dentist.
Treatments Keep the list of treatment types and prices current
Search existing treatments by name or click + Add Treatment to define a new one with its cost. These treatment types populate the dropdown used when registering appointments and calculating bills, so keep prices up to date.
Patients View and correct patient contact records
Every patient who has ever booked an appointment appears here. Search by name or contact number, then use the row Actions to edit a patient's address or contact details, for example if they moved or changed their phone number.
Staff Members Search staff accounts, or manage them if you're an Admin
Search staff by name, username or role to see who works at the clinic and what they do. If you're logged in as an Admin, a + Add New Staff button also appears here, letting you create, edit or remove staff accounts and assign roles like Coordinator, Receptionist, Nurse or Technologist.
Calculate & Print Bill Generate and print a receipt for a completed visit
Search a completed appointment by appointment number, patient name or contact number to bring up its receipt, which combines the treatment fee and consultation fee into a total amount due. Click Print Receipt to print a clean copy for the patient, or Cancel to go back to the list.
Notices Send a message directly to a dentist
Select the dentist you want to reach, type your message — a schedule change, reminder or general note — and click Send Notice. The notice appears in that dentist's portal and only they will see it.
Profile Settings Keep your own contact and account details up to date
Your Staff ID, role and username are fixed and shown for reference only. Update your full name or contact number at any time. To change your password, type a new one into the New Password field — leave it blank to keep your current password — then click Save Changes.
Log Out Safely end your session when you're done
Clears your current session completely and returns you to the login screen. Always log out when you're finished, especially on a shared or public computer, to keep patient and staff information secure.
