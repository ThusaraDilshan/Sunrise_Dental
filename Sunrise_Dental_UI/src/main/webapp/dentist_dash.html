Dentist Portal Guide

Click on any topic below to see more details
Today's Schedule A quick look at everyone booked in for today
Shows only today's appointments so you always know who's coming in next. Use the search bar to instantly filter by appointment number or patient name, and hit Refresh to pull the latest bookings. A banner at the top tells you at a glance how many appointments remain or lets you know when the day is all clear.
All Appointments Your full appointment history, past and upcoming
Lists every appointment ever assigned to you not just today's. Each row shows the patient's contact details, treatment type, date, time and a colour-coded status badge (Pending,Completed or Cancelled). Search by appointment number or patient name to find a specific visit in seconds.
Status Management Mark treatments as complete or cancel a booking
Right from the Actions column in Today's Schedule or My Appointments, click Complete once you've finished treating a patient or Cancel if the visit didn't go ahead. The status badge updates immediately and is reflected across the whole clinic system no page reload needed.
Search Appointment Pull up full details using just the appointment number
Type in an appointment number (e.g. APT0001 or Patient Name) to see the complete record patient name, address, contact number, treatment, dentist, date, time and current status without having to scroll through the full appointments table.
Notices Messages sent to you directly from the front desk
Any message the clinic staff send you schedule changes, reminders or general notes shows up here. Unread notices are highlighted and counted with a small red badge next to Notices in the sidebar, so you'll never miss one. Click a notice to mark it as read.
Reports A daily summary of your appointments and earnings
Pick any date and click Generate to see a breakdown of that day's appointments, completed treatments, cancellations and total earnings. Use Download PDF to save or print a copy of the report for your records.
Profile Settings Keep your contact and account details up to date
Update your full name, contact phone number, specialization and consultation fee at any time. To change your password, type a new one into the New Password field leave it blank if you'd rather keep your current password. Remember to click Save Changes when you're done.
Log Out Safely end your session when you're done
Clears your current session completely and returns you to the login screen. Always log out when you're finished, especially on a shared or public computer, to keep patient information secure.
